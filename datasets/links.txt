Datasets used (all public, downloaded automatically with kagglehub)
===================================================================

Task 1 - Image classification
  Garbage Classification (6 classes, ~2,500 images; derived from TrashNet, Yang & Thung 2016)
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/asdasdasasdas/garbage-classification
  kagglehub id: asdasdasasdas/garbage-classification

Task 2 - Text mining and clustering
  BBC Full Text Document Classification (2,225 news articles, 5 sections; Greene & Cunningham 2006)
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamkushwaha/bbc-full-text-document-classification
  kagglehub id: shivamkushwaha/bbc-full-text-document-classification

Task 3 - AI agent knowledge base
  TMDB 5000 Movie Dataset (tmdb_5000_movies.csv + tmdb_5000_credits.csv)
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tmdb/tmdb-movie-metadata
  kagglehub id: tmdb/tmdb-movie-metadata

Pre-trained models (downloaded from Hugging Face / Keras on first run)
  MobileNetV2 ImageNet weights          (Task 1, transfer-learning extension)
  sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (Tasks 2 and 3)
  sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2 (Task 2)
